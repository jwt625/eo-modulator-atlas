# Schema / skill proposals from batch p3_16 (not applied)

No sim configs written (graphene EAMs and rings, no dielectric traveling-wave device), so no sims/SPEC.md proposals.

- `eo_material = graphene_2d` fits all five papers; no gap. The mechanism differs (electro-absorption in lee2020, lee2020a, wu2023, luan2026a; electro-refraction in the transparency region in luan2026), same gap as p3_05 and p3_08: an `eo_effect` enum (pockels, plasma_dispersion, band_filling, franz_keldysh, pauli_blocking, electro_refraction_2d, ...) would label it.
- `device_class`: loss-modulated critically-coupled ring (lee2020) is entered as `ring` (batch hint `eam`); the schema has no way to say "ring whose modulation mechanism is absorption". Proposal: `mechanism` or `ring` + tag convention `eam` documented in the schema.
- `band` has no value for the 2 um band (luan2026-b, luan2026a-a use `other`; the paper calls it "mid-infrared"). Propose `two_um` (or `swir`), and clarify that `mid_ir` is not meant for 2 um.
- `integration` has no value for wet/dry-transferred 2D materials on a planarized waveguide (all five papers use `other`; wu2023 uses `foundry_native` because the 300 mm flow is run in a CMOS pilot line). Propose `transferred_2d`.
- EAM metrics have no columns: modulation efficiency / static absorption change in dB/um or dB/mm (luan2026a 0.22 and 0.2 dB/um; wu2023 modulation depth 50 +- 4 dB/mm; normalized IL 87 dB/mm), voltage for 3 dB modulation (lee2020a dV3dB 4.50 V), extinction ratio quoted for a stated drive amplitude (wu2023 ER at 6 Vpp, luan2026 OMA at 3 V swing) and optical modulation amplitude in dBm (luan2026 -1.97 and -3.36 dBm). Kept in notes. Propose `mod_efficiency_db_per_um`, `dv_3db_v`, `er_drive_vpp_v`, `oma_dbm`.
- `drive_vpp_v` is documented as the system-demo amplitude; the S21/ER measurement amplitudes (lee2020 Vpp 3 V, wu2023 6 Vpp, 1 V DC bias for S21) overlap the same meaning. Lee2020 and luan2026 enter the eye-diagram/S21 amplitude there; the rule should be stated.
- Population statistics have no columns (mean +- std, number of devices, yield): wu2023 (400 devices, yield above 95%), lee2020a (32 modulators, 94% yield, 3.92 +- 0.53 GHz), luan2026-c (10 devices). Entered as the mean with the std in notes; propose `n_devices`, `yield_pct`, `*_std` or a `statistic` text column (mean/median/best).
- Mean vs median: wu2023 Tables 2-3 give mean +- std while the text quotes medians (ER 1.3 vs 1.2 dB, BW 15.3 vs 15.1 GHz); no column or basis value tells which statistic a cell is.
- Simulated design rows inside a measured paper (lee2020a-b optimized HfO2 design, ER 15 dB and IL 1 dB, basis `simulated`): the convention (d) row rule does not say whether a hypothetical device gets a row. A `simulated_design` tag was used; propose documenting it and excluding such rows from measured-only views.
- Temperature: `temperature_class = cryogenic` loses the actual temperature (lee2020: 4.9 K vs 293 K rows). Propose `temperature_k`.
- `bw3db_reference = other` plus `bw3db_reference_freq_ghz` is used for lee2020 (curves normalized at 1 MHz and a single-pole fit gives the 3 dB point; the fit, not the data crossing, defines the value). No basis value distinguishes "from a fit to measured data" (used `measured` with a note).
- `vpi_convention = resonance_tuning_derived` is used for luan2026-a where the authors give only a pi phase change at 22 V for 10 um and V_pi L = 220 V um with no stated extraction method; the convention is an inference recorded in the evidence note. A pi phase on a slot-waveguide phase shifter in a ring is not the ring's own V_pi.
- Insertion-loss definition for resonant and EAM devices quoted from normalized spectra (luan2026, luan2026a, lee2020a): `il_onchip_db` was filled with `il_onchip_excludes` = "definition not stated". The skill should say whether an undefined IL belongs in `il_onchip_db`.
- Inter-section inconsistencies in preprints (luan2026a: dielectric 50 nm in design vs 35 nm in Methods; BW 22 vs 40 GHz; luan2026: coupling length 5 vs 10 um; lee2020a: top Al2O3 30 vs 40 nm in the figure) have no home besides notes; a `data_quality_flags` field would let views surface them.
- Skill/instruction: BATCH_INSTRUCTIONS text says `verified_on` 2026-10-01 while this task said 2026-10-02 (used). Crossref for lee2020 lists publisher "Wiley" for a 10.1515 DOI (title and eight authors match); a rule for such publisher mismatches would help.

## Audit follow-up (2026-10-03)
- The earlier `bw3db_reference = other` bullet (a single-pole fit defines the lee2020 3 dB value, no basis value for it) is resolved in the rows with basis `derived` (audit F6); no new schema request, but the skill could state that a 3 dB point read from a fitted transfer function is `derived`, not `measured`.
- Rows whose only cell for a quantity is empty (luan2026-b and luan2026a-b IL) must not carry a `*_basis` value; a validator check "basis column set while all headline cells of that group are empty" would catch this (audit F14).
- Paper-level fields (`published_on`, `license`, `redistribution`, `source_type`) have no evidence entry type: `validate_db.py` rejects entries whose `field` is not a devices column, so the "paper's own notice quoted in the evidence note" of convention (k) has no home except `papers.csv` notes. Proposal: allow `device_id: paper` entries for papers columns.
