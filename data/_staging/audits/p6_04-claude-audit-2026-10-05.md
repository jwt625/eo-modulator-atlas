Eindhoven University of Technology ROR `02c2kyt77` is verified (7 occurrences in `references/hillier2025/crossref.json`).
2. **hillier2025 per-quadrature rows and emptied contradictions; the 80 GHz EE value.**
   - Row split per quadrature point is correct under (d)/(z) (Vpi, ER and bias differ per point; shared physical_device_id; geometry repeated).
   - The three emptied cells are correct under (y), and the figures give further support: (a) 2 mm Q1 bias: text "6 V and 7 V" (p.6) vs the Q1 line at about 3.6 V in Fig. 2(b), and Fig. 2(d) plots the VpiL 1.4 V cm point (7.0 V x 2 mm) at about 3.6 V; (b) 1 mm Q2 ER: text 17.0 dB vs Fig. 2(a) label 15.4, Fig. 2(c) point 15.4 at 13.4 V, and the trace (max about -8.9, min about -24.3 dBm) about 15.4 dB; (c) 2 mm Q1 ER: text 15.4 dB vs Fig. 2(b) label 16.1 and trace about 16 dB. The data-run bias (text "about 13 V" vs Q* arrows at about 9.3-12 V (1 mm) and 7.2-9.8 V (2 mm)) is also correctly left empty.
   - The 80 +/- 8 GHz value does not belong in bw6db_ghz: finding N1. It is an absolute electrical-electrical S21 (Fig. 3(a) axis "Transmitted electrical power", -6 dB line), not an EO response; precedent puts electrical-S21 statements in notes (qi2024-a "Electrical S21 above -6.41 dB to 110 GHz" in notes; DevLog-020 g4: lin2026a-a eo_rolloff "from electrical S-parameters" moved to notes). The EO inset (about 90 GHz) is adapted from ref. [25] (thesis) and its measured trace shows no 3 dB crossing: correctly not entered ((bb) earlier work -> notes).
3. **horst2025-pm (IL 5.6 dB and VpiL 234 V um of "the 10-um-long phase modulator").** Keep as its own row. The p.2 sentence follows the general device description and names no device; Devices 1 and 2 differ only in pad size and both have the 10 um slot (p.2 "10 um slot length", p.3 "2Cslot ... 10 um length ... measured THz modulator"), so the numbers are plausibly from one of them or a sibling, but copying one measurement onto both rows would assert two measurements. Scope `undefined` is right under (s) (coupler loss 2.9 dB/coupler stated separately, but the IL definition is not given). per_arm_phase_shifter and the MZM value 117 V um left in notes are correct. No fix required.
4. **singer2025 4.2 dB on-chip IL.** The value is stated by the authors twice ("the optical on-chip excess loss is 4.2 dB", p.6 and p.8); it equals the Table 2 (p.17) sum of the current on-chip elements (0.7 + 2.8 + 0.5 + 0.2 dB) and equals 10.8 - 2 x 3.3 dB (PWB loss "estimated", p.16). Basis `derived` in the evidence entry is correct under (h) (the authors deduce it from cutback test structures and an estimated PWB loss), and `il_onchip_scope device_total` is correct under (s): it covers the whole device (strip routing, MMIs, S2S converters, slot section) with only the fiber-chip interfaces removed; it is not relative to a reference waveguide. Only the row-note wording needs a touch (m7).
