## 2. Findings

### Numerical

**F1 (numerical). li2022b-a: Vpi 3.3 V (and VpiL 1.1 V cm) is the authors' label, but the plotted sweep in Fig. 6(a) spans about 2.75 V.**
- Row/field: `li2022b-a.vpi_dc_v = 3.3` (qualifier approx), `vpil_dc_vcm = 1.1`; evidence entry cites "Fig. 6(a)".
- Source: p.5 text "Vpi of ~3.3 V" and Fig. 6(a) arrow label "3.3 V". In a 500 dpi render of Fig. 6(a) the dashed markers sit on the transmission maximum at about 0.95 V and the minimum at about 3.70 V (axis ticks 0-4 V), i.e. a span of about 2.75 V, which would give VpiL = 2.75 x 0.33 = 0.91 V cm, close to the paper's own simulated 0.96 V cm, whereas the abstract's 1.1 V cm follows the 3.3 V label. The paper is internally inconsistent; the CSV copies the text value, which is the right primary entry.
- Suggested correction: keep 3.3 V approx as the authors' stated value but add to the evidence note and row notes: "Fig. 6(a) axis reads about 2.75 V between the marked extrema; label says 3.3 V; VpiL would be about 0.91 V cm". Do not describe the evidence as read from Fig. 6(a) without this caveat. Also the evidence for `vpil_dc_vcm` says "authors' product from 3.3 V and 3.3 mm"; the paper only states "1.1 V cm" in the abstract with no derivation (see F9).

**F2 (numerical). zhang2022-a/b: `z0_ohm = 41.5` at 250 GHz is the text value; Fig. 2(b) shows about 43.5 ohm at 250 GHz.**
- Source: p.5 "The extracted Z_C is 41.5 ohm at 250 GHz ... close to the simulated result (40.5 ohm)". In Fig. 2(b) (cached `img_p04_2.png`) the measured markers rise to about 43.6 ohm at 230 GHz and read about 43.4 ohm at 250 GHz; the simulated curve reads about 40.6 ohm at 250 GHz (matches the text). The measured trace is about 41-41.5 ohm near 140-150 GHz. The evidence entry cites Fig. 2(b) as support, which it does not at 250 GHz. The sim config also uses 41.5 +/- 2.0 as a target (a +/- 2 band still covers the figure value).
- Suggested correction: keep 41.5 (paper text) but add to evidence/row notes "Fig. 2(b) measured markers read about 43.4 ohm at 250 GHz; text 41.5 ohm" and drop Fig. 2(b) as the sole locator, or mark the value `extracted_from_figure` ~43 with approx if the figure is preferred. Also, one curve is shown for an unspecified length; the entry on both rows is disclosed as "common cross-section" (acceptable).

**F3 (numerical). zhang2022-a/b bandwidths: the 3 dB and 6 dB crossings are read from the model-calculated EO S21 curve; the evidence note "smoothed sideband data" is not supported by Fig. 5(c,d).**
- Source: Fig. 5(c) (10.8 mm) and 5(d) (5.8 mm): the raw blue circles scatter by about +/-3 dB; the only continuous trace in these two panels is the red "calculated values" curve, which crosses -3 dB near 100 / 170 GHz and -6 dB near 175 / 295 GHz, matching the text. The caption mentions dashed smoothed lines, but they are visible only in panels (a,b) (RF Vpi). The text (p.6) says "our measurement results indicate" these bandwidths and the abstract says "measured"; the CSV `bw_basis = measured` follows the authors.
- Suggested correction: keep the values and `measured` (authors' claim) but change the evidence notes to "crossing read on the EO S21 curve calculated from measured s-parameters, which the authors state agrees with the raw sideband data (scatter +/-3 dB)"; and in the 5.8 mm row note state that the raw data are scattered. Replace "smoothed sideband data" and the row note "values read from smoothed trace (Fig. 5)".

**F4 (numerical). churaev2023-c: `prop_loss_db_per_cm = 0.085` is a best-resonance value, entered beside `wavelength_nm = 1552` (comb centre) with no qualifier and no wavelength of its own.**
- Source: p.3 "intrinsic quality factors ... up to Q = 4.5 x 10^6. The latter corresponds to ... 8.5 dB/m"; Fig. 2(g) shows the linewidth/loss depends on optical frequency (the paper itself says losses rise with frequency), so 8.5 dB/m is at the best frequency, not at 1552 nm, and "hundreds of resonances above 4e6" describes the distribution. Row/evidence note do say "best resonance, 21 GHz ring" in the evidence only; the CSV row note says "Intrinsic Q up to 4.5e6" but pairs the loss with the 1552 nm wavelength cell.
- Suggested correction: add to the row notes "8.5 dB/m is the lowest-loss (highest-Q) resonance; wavelength of that resonance not stated; 1552 nm is the EO-comb centre wavelength", and consider qualifier `prop_loss_db_per_cm:approx` is not appropriate but the "up to" wording makes the value a floor of the loss distribution; a note is sufficient.

**F5 (numerical). Sidewall-angle reference axis differs from other rows: liu2023-a..d `sidewall_angle_deg = 30` is "relative to the vertical axis"; celik2022-a `12` has no stated axis.**
- Source: liu2023 p.4 "sidewall angle of 30 deg (relative to the vertical axis)"; celik2022 p.4 "etch angle constraint theta_etch = 12 deg", Fig. 2 caption "theta_etch is the sidewall angle" (axis not stated; the inset cannot be resolved). A plot or filter on `sidewall_angle_deg` will place liu2023 near vertical-etch devices' opposite end.
- Suggested correction: for liu2023 enter 60 with an evidence note "authors give 30 deg from vertical; converted to 60 deg from the substrate plane" (a derived entry with formula 90 - 30), or leave the field empty and keep the 30 deg in the stack text; for celik2022 leave empty (axis unstated) or keep with an explicit "axis not stated, do not compare" note in the CSV `notes` (the evidence already says it). A schema line fixing the convention would remove the ambiguity (propose in SPEC_PROPOSALS).

**F6 (numerical). xu2020-a: `qualifiers = bw3db_ghz:gt;bw3db_ghz:approx` contradict each other and `bw_measured_to_ghz` is empty although the sweep to 67 GHz is the range.**
- Source: p.5 "greater than 48 GHz with a reference frequency of 1.5 GHz"; Fig. 4(a) marks a crossing at "~48 GHz" (verified: the 13 mm trace crosses -3 dB near 48 GHz and reaches -5 dB at 67 GHz); Table 1 "~ 48". - Suggested correction: since the crossing is observed at about 48 GHz, enter `bw3db_ghz:approx` only (or `gt` only, following the text, but not both) and fill `bw_measured_to_ghz = 67` for xu2020-a.
