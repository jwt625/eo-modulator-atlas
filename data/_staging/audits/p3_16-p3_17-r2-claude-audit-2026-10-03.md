## Findings

### Numerical

**R2-F1 (numerical). lotkov2024-a `bw3db_ghz` 1.05 and `bw6db_ghz` 2.3 are fit-defined but labelled `measured`.**
- Cells: `data/devices.csv` lotkov2024-a `bw3db_ghz` 1.05, `bw6db_ghz` 2.3, `bw_basis` measured. `data/evidence/lotkov2024.yaml` entries for `bw3db_ghz` and `bw6db_ghz` have basis measured.
- Source: p.4 "Fitting the response at 1550 nm indicate 3dB bandwidth limitation of 1.05 GHz and 6dB bandwidth limitation of 2.3 GHz".
- Fig. 4(d) (my reading of img_p04_1, approximate): the noisy normalized S21 first touches -3 dB near 0.9-1.0 GHz. It dips below -6 dB already near 1.5 GHz, recovers above -3 dB around 1.8-2.2 GHz, and stays mostly below -6 dB beyond 2.3 GHz. No fit curve is drawn, only the two dashed markers.
- Why it matters: this is the same situation round 1 F6 corrected for lee2020 and lee2020a (3 dB point defined by a fit, raw trace not monotonic). Leaving it measured makes lotkov2024 inconsistent with those rows.
- Change: set `bw_basis` = derived on lotkov2024-a, and set evidence basis = derived for `bw3db_ghz` and `bw6db_ghz`. Add to the evidence notes "fit of normalized S21 (p.4); raw trace noisy, crosses -6 dB near 1.5 GHz (approx. reading)". Values unchanged.
