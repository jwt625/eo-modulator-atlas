## Rulings on the distiller's judgment calls (BATCH_REPORT)

Transducer rows (mohl2025, axline2026) and convention (dd)
- (dd) reads: "no device row when the paper has no device-level metric in scope (transducers, SSB/IQ architecture without Vpi/BW/IL/ER, electrode-only test structures, reviews/models, results cited from earlier work)". The parenthetical lists typical papers that lack an in-scope device metric; it is not a categorical ban. The p8 DISTILL_PROMPT makes this explicit: "A device used as an EO modulator inside a larger experiment (transducer, ...) gets rows only for the modulator metrics the paper reports for it." Precedents: holzgrafe2020 and multani2025 (audited) are papers-only because no numeric modulator-type quantity exists (holzgrafe2020 notes: tuning "shown only as detuning plots ... with no nm/V or GHz/V number"); witmer2020 (audited) has transducer rows carrying numeric EO tuning (nm/V), loaded Q and FSR; eltes2020-a carries r_eff plus loss only.
- axline2026: rows upheld. Conversions verified (lambda^2/c x value at the Table B.1 wavelength): tuning 0.00256, 0.00146, 0.00245, 0.00203, 0.00296, 0.00274 nm/V; FSR 0.504, 0.562, 0.594, 0.561, 0.593, 0.593 nm, all correct to the stated digits.
