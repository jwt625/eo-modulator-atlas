### Medium

**F1 (medium). `bw3db_reference` is blank in devices.csv while evidence, BATCH_REPORT and DevLog-010 assert dc; the bandwidth convention is not in the row.**
- Location: `data/_staging/p2_02/devices.csv` (`bw3db_reference` empty; `notes` has no bandwidth convention); `evidence/ummethala2021.yaml` entry bw3db_reference = dc; BATCH_REPORT judgment call 4 and ledger; DevLog-010 "Staged results".
- Source: p.4 (3 dB = index 1/sqrt2 = 3 dB of detected RF power), p.14 Eq. (S21) (normalized to zero angular frequency), Fig. 2(d) axis 0 dB at the lowest frequency.
- Evidence: my mechanical comparison flagged the entry as pointing at an empty cell; `bw3db_reference` is not an evidence-required column, so the validator did not catch it. The CSV therefore reads as "reference unknown"; 6 canonical rows and p2_01 use the same blank/`unspecified` for genuinely unreferenced values, so a reader or view cannot tell this row from them. The detected-RF-power scale appears only in an evidence note.
- Correction: set `bw3db_reference = dc` (the evidence basis is `derived`, keep it) and add one short sentence to the row `notes`: "3 dB on detected RF power (index down by 1/sqrt2), referenced to zero frequency, 50 ohm terminated, PD/probes de-embedded; 6 dB = photocurrent power down by 4". Correct the "row note states it" sentence in BATCH_REPORT (or make it true).

**F2 (medium). Convention (b) qualifiers/provenance: `wavelength_nm` and `electrode_thickness_um`.**
- Location: devices.csv `wavelength_nm` 1550 (qualifier absent), evidence entry `wavelength_nm` (basis design_target, locator p.12 Eq. S13) and `band` c_band; `electrode_thickness_um` 0.15 (evidence note itself says "approximately 150 nm on p.4").
- Source: p.12 Eq.
