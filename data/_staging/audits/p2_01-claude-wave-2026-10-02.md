## 4. 6 dB EOE convention, Wolf (checklist 4)

The paper explicitly uses the 6 dB point of the electro-optic-electric (EOE) response (p.7 and Fig. 3 caption, p.16) and states that this corresponds to a halving of the optical power and photocurrent amplitude, measured as a four-fold (6 dB) drop of spectral power density by the VNA. The batch kept the value out of `bw3db_ghz`, left `bw3db_reference` unspecified and put 25 GHz in `bw6db_ghz` with a note. No bandwidth value is recorded under a wrong convention, and the choice not to convert is defensible. The schema conventions (a)-(k) have no letter covering EOE/optical-power/electrical-power dB scales: (c) concerns crossings and measurement limits, (g) concerns roll-off, so the batch could not tag "per the convention letters" beyond (c) (no crossing problem: the 6 dB crossing is observed, `bw_measured_to_ghz` correctly empty since the trace ends at about 55 GHz, Fig. 3(c)). Residual issues: F2 (documentation of the paper's own equivalence; a visible 3 dB electrical-power crossing near 8-9 GHz that must not be read as the headline) and F8 (reference set while no 3 dB value).

Kieninger's 40 GHz is stated as "3 dB EO bandwidth" with no definition of the dB scale; same research group, possibly the same practice, but the paper does not say. `unspecified` is right.

**F2 (medium). Wolf 25 GHz is entered as `bw6db_ghz` without recording the paper's own statement of what a 6 dB EOE drop means, and the field's cross-paper meaning is undefined.**
- Location: wolf2018a-gated `bw6db_ghz`, evidence note, notes; SPEC_PROPOSALS item 5.
- Source: p.7 and Fig. 3 caption (p.16): the bandwidth of an EO modulator is "usually specified" at a 6 dB drop of the EOE response, which for the modulation spectral component means optical power (and photocurrent amplitude) halved, shown as a four-fold power-spectral-density drop at the VNA. Fig. 3(c): the EOE amplitude trace crosses -3 dB at about 8-9 GHz and -6 dB at about 22-30 GHz (noisy plateau), data end near 55 GHz.
- Evidence: the entry note says "Six-decibel EOE response drop ... not a three-decibel response entry" but omits the optical-power equivalence, so a reader cannot tell that the authors regard this as their standard EO bandwidth. In the atlas, `bw6db_ghz` is also used by other papers (li2026ba 100 GHz next to a 64 GHz 3 dB value, ogiso2024, deng2026, qiu2026), where it is a second threshold on the same response; Wolf's value is a single threshold on a different dB scale (power-spectral-density at the VNA). A "6 dB bandwidth" plot or column would place them side by side without that distinction. The 3 dB (electrical-power, VNA) crossing near 8-9 GHz exists only in the figure and is correctly not entered, but nothing warns that it is not the headline.
- Correction: keep the value in `bw6db_ghz` (no conversion invented) but add the equivalence statement and the Fig. 3(c) locator to the evidence note and the device note (short paraphrase, no quotation), and ask D2/U2 to label the field or exclude `convention=EOE` rows from plots that mix 6 dB columns until the schema defines the dB scale (SPEC_PROPOSALS 5 already asks for it).

**F3 (medium). `drive_vpp_v` has different reference planes in the two papers, and Kieninger's 1.44 Vpp is stated only for PAM4 but attached to a row that also lists OOK.**
- Location: kieninger2020-open-ended `drive_vpp_v` = 1.44, `modulation_format` = "OOK;PAM4"; wolf2018a-gated `drive_vpp_v` = 1.4.
- Source: Kieninger p.7-8: "for a 100 GBd PAM4 signal" the swing at the amplifier output into 50 ohm is 0.78 Vpp, 0.72 Vpp after the 0.7 dB probe loss at 50 GHz, and the open-end reflection doubles it to an effective 1.44 Vpp. Wolf p.6 and Fig. 2(c) (p.15): the RF amplifier output swing is 1.4 Vpp, then coupled through probes into a terminated line (no probe-loss correction stated).
- Evidence: Kieninger's cell is a derived effective voltage at the open-ended device after probe loss and doubling; Wolf's is the amplifier-output (source-side) swing into a matched termination. The notes and evidence of the Kieninger row spell the chain out (0.78, 0.72, 1.44), but the Wolf row note does not name its plane ("External 50-ohm load is not measured Z0" only). A column of `drive_vpp_v` would compare an effective-at-device number with a source-plane number. For OOK, the paper gives no swing, yet the row combines OOK and PAM4 with one 1.44 Vpp.
- Correction: (1) add "amplifier output, before probe, terminated line" to the Wolf-gated device note; (2) in the Kieninger-open-ended device note and evidence note, say the 0.78 / 0.72 / 1.44 chain is stated for 100 GBd PAM4 only and that the OOK drive amplitude is not stated; (3) ask D2/U2 not to rank `drive_vpp_v` across rows without a plane tag (SPEC_PROPOSALS 6).

**F4 (medium). Table 1 central values below their own spread, and the paper-level ER of 30 dB copied to all 16 rows, will rank as best-in-class unless views guard them.**
- Location: devices.csv `il_onchip_db` and `extinction_ratio_db` of the 16 die rows; SPEC_PROPOSALS item 3.
- Source: Table 1 (p.10): Die 4 MZM 3 a_PS = 0.01 +/- 0.36 dB, Die 3 MZM 3 = 0.03 +/- 0.31 dB, Die 3 MZM 4 = 0.21 +/- 0.32 dB; p.5: "extinction ratio of around 30 dB ... consistent for all investigated devices" (stated once, in the paragraph on one typical device, Fig. 3(a)).
- Evidence: the cells are exact transcriptions and the notes carry the standard deviation, but `il_onchip_db` is a single float with scope only in free text (includes/excludes); the ranking/Pareto logic (`vpi_il_vdb`, lowest-loss sorts, `repKey`) sees 0.01 dB and 0.03 dB as the lowest "on-chip insertion loss" in the database, with a different scope from whole-chip on-chip loss (excludes MMIs and mode converters, unlike chen2022 and kharel2021 style entries) and with spreads larger than the value. Separately, 16 identical `extinction_ratio_db = 30` (basis measured, approx) cells state a paper-level sentence as 16 independent measurements; the paper gives no per-sample ER.
- Correction: either (a) keep the cells (they are faithful) and add a machine-visible guard in D2/U2 (exclude `statistical_replicate`-tagged rows from loss rankings and from `representative` selection, treat the loss as phase-only), or (b) drop `extinction_ratio_db` from the 15 replicate rows other than one typical device and leave one entry with a paper-level note. Do not enter any lower bound for the near-zero values. Whichever is chosen, record it in D2's integration note.
