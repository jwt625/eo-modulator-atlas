## Findings

### zhang2023

F1 (numerical) zhang2023-a: q_loaded 9066, il_onchip_db 1.3, extinction_ratio_db 32 describe the electrode-free cavity of Fig. 2d (p.5), but the 104 GHz row is the S21 device. p.6 text: "we select another topological modulator device on the same chip with a lower Q factor of 5400 ... limited to 35 GHz", and the 37 GHz bandwidth at -3 dB detuning matches that 35 GHz photon-lifetime limit, so the S21 device is most probably the Q 5400 device. The row note says "Q of the S21 device not stated", which understates this. Correction: state in the row note that p.6 implies the S21 device has Q 5400 (photon lifetime 4.5 ps, 35 GHz calculated), keep 9066 only as the electrode-free device value with that caveat, and record the RC-limited bandwidth "beyond 1.1 THz" (p.5, derivation in S6) and the 35 GHz limit in notes.

F2 (minor) zhang2023-a il_onchip_db: the paper gives "insertion loss 1.3 dB" (p.5) without saying whether grating couplers are normalized out; the batch fills the on-chip field with empty il_onchip_includes/excludes. Per SKILL rule 3 either leave empty or add "definition not stated" in includes/excludes. Fix: add the caveat.

F3 (minor) zhang2023-a wavelength_nm 1560 approx: read from Fig. 2e (device with electrodes; 0 V peak between ticks 1559.18 and 1560.04, near 1559.6 by eye). The Q 9066 / 0.172 nm numbers imply 1559.4 nm for the Fig. 2d device. 1560 approx is within the stated approximation; the note should say the wavelength belongs to the Fig. 2e tuning device, not necessarily the S21 device.
