## Rulings on the coordinator's points

1. eltes2020-c vpi_dc_v empty: accept. p.3 text: "pi phase shift ... 6 x 10^6 V m-1 ... ~50 V, and VpiL of 5 V cm". Supplement p.15 Note 7: "For a full pi phase shift +/-50 V are applied". Fig. 4b (p.4, opened) switches across -6 to +6 MV/m, about +/-54 V at 9 um. VpiL 5 V cm at 0.5 mm means a 100 V swing, so "~50 V" is the per-polarity amplitude, not a one-sided Vpi. Entering 50 would contradict the VpiL, and 100 V is never printed. vpil_dc_vcm 5, mzm_single_arm, single_ended and an empty vpi_dc_freq_ghz (true DC sweep) are correct. Optionally the coordinator may enter vpi_dc_v = 100 as derived (2 x 50 V, SI Note 7) with a derived-list entry; the auditor prefers empty.
2. eltes2020-a r_eff on the SiN row: accept, with the stronger evidence in F9. Value 200 pm/V at 45 deg, 4 K, read from Fig. 2b (p.3, opened: 4 K peak about 200, error bar about 175-230; 300 K peak about 365). approx is justified ("around 200", p.3). Basis derived (hysteresis fit, SI Eq. 3) is correct.
3. eltes2020-b bw3db 30 approx: see F2. The -6 dB EOE marker and the stated 30 GHz setup limit coincide. Add bw_measured_to_ghz = 30 in every case, and use bw6db_ghz for the -6 dB marker (wolf2018a/zwickel2020 precedent).
4. eltes2023-a / li2024-c mixed drive amplitudes in one row: accept a single row. Conventions (d) and (z) make a drive swing alone no reason for a new row (Vpi, IL and bandwidth are shared); (z) needs a note, and both rows have one. drive_vpp_v = 0.83 V correctly belongs to the Table 1 / Table II operating point that also defines energy_per_bit_fj, under (r). The notes say clearly that max_net_rate_gbps (262 / 300) was measured at 3 / 3.7 Vpp. Arithmetic checked: Vrms = (0.83/2)/sqrt(2) = 0.293 V, P = 1.72 mW, (1.72 + 0.1) mW / 200 Gb/s = 9.1 fJ/bit.
5. li2024-o empty Vpi/IL: accept (F8 adds only length and Z0). The "applicable to both bands" sentence covers design parameters. The 3.6 V and about 2 dB are measurements whose wavelength is not stated, and eltes2023 (C-band only) ties them to the C-band device.
6. eltes2023-b and li2024-ps as one structure in two papers: keep both rows (F7). Correct the convention (F4).
7. kohli2023 RF Vpi 9.6 V frequency: accept in vpi_rf_v with vpi_rf_freq_ghz empty. p.5 calls it the "high frequency half-wave voltage" from the ratio of the first and second sidebands (Eq. 3), which needs optically resolved sidebands, and compares it with the regime above the 1-10 GHz drop-off of Fig. 5a. That places it above 1 GHz (convention m) without a stated frequency. The vpi_convention change is F3.
