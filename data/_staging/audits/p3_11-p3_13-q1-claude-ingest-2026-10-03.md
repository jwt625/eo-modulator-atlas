F1 (numerical, highest priority) kari2025-a `bw3db_ghz` 29, basis `measured`.
Paper: Methods p.10-11 list a Siglent VNA covering "100 KHz to 7.5 GHz" and a 12 GHz photodetector for the EO response; p.5 says Fig. 2e bandwidths are "estimated" from the extracted Q and that VNA measurements on (W 0.75, L 100) and (W 0.55, L 100) "closely align"; Fig. 2e caption: "3 dB modulation bandwidth of our resonators calculated using the Q-factor", and the 29 GHz and 4.4 GHz triangles lie on the Q-derived curves; Fig. 4c (p.8) plots the normalised EO response of SR 1 out to about 60 GHz with the -3 dB crossing near 25-30 GHz. Photon-lifetime check with Eqs. (1)-(3) and Q 6232 at about 1566 nm: tau = Q/omega = 5.2 ps, f = 1/(2 pi tau) = 30.6 GHz, i.e. the 29 GHz equals the Q-limited value, and a 7.5 GHz VNA cannot have observed a 29 GHz crossing. Problem: the evidence entry and CSV carry basis `measured` for a number the paper's own Methods cannot support as measured; the conflict is only in a free-text note. SKILL rule 2 / common pitfalls (simulated or Q-derived bandwidth entered as measured).
Correction: keep value 29 but set the evidence basis (and `bw_basis` on row a) to `derived` or `author_estimate` with the Eq. 1-3 formula in the `derived:` list, or keep `measured` only if the coordinator resolves the Methods conflict; keep row b `measured`. State the source conflict in the evidence note, not only in the CSV note. I cannot settle from the cached text which is true.

Correction: leave `wavelength_nm` empty on rows c and d (or enter the text order with basis `extracted_from_figure`) and record the three-way conflict (text, table, Fig. 4) in the evidence note. The V_pi/2 numbers (18 V, 35 V) are not affected. Medium confidence on my figure reading; the coordinator should confirm on Fig. 4(b),(c).
