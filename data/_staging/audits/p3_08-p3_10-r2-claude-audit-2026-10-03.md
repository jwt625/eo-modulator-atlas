### Numerical

**R2-F1 (numerical). ulrich2025-mzi `vpil_dc_vcm` = 1.04 is the meander-compensated value, not the VpiL of the measured device; the row does not say so.**
- Cell: `data/devices.csv` ulrich2025-mzi `vpil_dc_vcm` = 1.04 (`vpi_basis` derived); evidence entry vpil_dc_vcm (locator "p.6 text; p.22 Eq. 22", note "author-computed from r_eff, confinement and group index").
- Source: p.22 Supplement 4.2, Eq. 20-22: r_eff,meas (the phase shift divided by the full 15.37 mm length) is about 280 pm/V; the reported r_eff = r_eff,meas / 0.81 = 345 pm/V corrects for the meander sections at 45/135/225 deg and the bends; Eq. 22 then computes VpiL = lambda d / (Gamma n_o^2 n_g r_eff) = 1.04 +/- 0.08 V cm with r_eff = 345 pm/V. p.6 main text: "a voltage change of 0.084 V/um results in a pi phase shift". For this device: 0.084 V/um x 10 um = 0.84 V, x 1.537 cm = 1.29 V cm; equivalently 1.04 x 345/280 = 1.28 V cm. So 1.04 V cm is the product for a hypothetical device with all of its length at the favourable 45 deg orientation; the meandered MZI that was measured has about 1.29 V cm (my arithmetic, about 24 % higher).
- Why numerical: the row is the device row and a VpiL view will plot 1.04 as this device; the row note says "Vpi*L is the author-computed value for the effective Pockels coefficient r_eff 345" but not that 345 is the geometry-corrected value nor what the device itself achieves.
- Proposed change: keep `vpil_dc_vcm` = 1.04 (the reported value, SKILL rule 4). Add to the row notes: "1.04 V cm uses the meander-corrected r_eff 345 pm/V (= r_eff,meas about 280 pm/V / 0.81, Supplement Eq. 20-22); for the measured meandered device, 0.084 V/um per pi (p.6) x 10 um x 15.37 mm gives about 1.29 V cm." Add the same to the evidence note (within 25 words, e.g. "uses meander-corrected r_eff 345 pm/V; device as measured about 1.29 V cm (0.084 V/um x 10 um x 1.537 cm)") and an evidence `derived` item {device_id: ulrich2025-mzi, field: vpil_dc_vcm, value: 1.29, formula: "E_pi x d x L = 0.084 V/um x 10 um x 1.537 cm", inputs: [electrode_gap_um, length_mm]} so the as-measured figure is traceable. Optional: tag `meander_corrected_vpil`.
