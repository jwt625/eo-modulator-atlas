**F1 (minor) li2025b-a / li2025b-b `optical_input_power_dbm` (+10 dBm, -14 dBm).** Source: Fig. 13(b) caption p.11 gives 10 dBm for the broadband scans of "two PeM-MZM devices with different arm length differences" (1.4 mm and 3 mm), and Fig. 13(a) caption gives -14 dBm for the racetrack broadband scan. **F2 (minor) li2025b-a `vpi_basis` = measured, `vpi_dc_v` = 54.3.** Value verified (p.4: 54.3 +/- 1.3 V; racetrack 31.9 +/- 0.8 V; SeM controls 86 V and 93 V, p.5). D31-D33 p.14-15), not a read-out of a DC half-wave; the authors say the DC sweep (Fig. 3(d)) can "also" give Vpi but print no value. Row b (same fit method) is labelled derived while row a is measured: inconsistent under convention (h). Suggest `derived` for a (or `measured` for both with a note). The CSV records the authors' label (push-pull, mzm_push_pull) faithfully; add a note that the convention is the authors' and was not independently confirmed.
