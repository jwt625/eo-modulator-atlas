## Scope and unfinished search branches

- Years use journal/conference year for a published work and submission year for a preprint-only work. Thus a 2019-online/2020-issue paper can be in scope. Exact arXiv versions and publication dates are separate fields.
- Existing stable IDs with a 2025 suffix can refer to journal articles issued in 2026. Do not rename those IDs automatically.
- Integrated LN, LT, BTO, EO polymers, III-V and silicon comparison devices are core. Stress-optic PZT, free-space vertical-cavity devices and material-coefficient studies are marked as auxiliary in the manifest.
- Further independent search is still useful for 2020–2022 InP/NTT and silicon foundries, backward/forward citations, recently accepted 2026 papers and conference-to-journal pairs. Unidentified entries on group lists are leads, not new candidate rows.
- Public supplementary data and dataset packages have not been comprehensively collected. DOI-linked datasets should be inspected before asking the user to retrieve them.
- `search_log.json` records the later verbatim search batches. This route map also summarizes the early batches. The queue files record the exact acquisition URLs and routes; `manifest.csv` records the final per-paper state.
