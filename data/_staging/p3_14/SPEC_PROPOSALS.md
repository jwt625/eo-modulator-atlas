# Schema / skill proposals from batch p3_14 (not applied)

- Sideband-suppression and SSB/comb metrics (chen2024, guo2026-b) have no columns; chen2024 therefore has no device rows although it is a modulator architecture paper. Proposal: a free-text `system_metrics` column or a companion table keyed by device_id, or an explicit rule that SSB/IQ-architecture papers without Vpi/BW/IL/ER get a papers.csv row only.
- Electrode-only test-structure papers (sayem2026): no EO material and no optical waveguide, so `eo_material` (required) cannot be filled and the RF loss coefficient is in dB/cm/sqrt(GHz) (no column; `rf_loss_db_per_cm` needs a frequency). Proposal: allow device_class `electrode_test` with `eo_material` empty, plus `rf_loss_coeff_db_per_cm_sqrtghz`, or keep such papers as papers.csv-only rows by rule.
- Upconverted / multi-wavelength outputs of one chip (sabatti2024 FH and SH): represented as two rows differing by wavelength and ER; the SH row shares geometry columns. Proposal: state in the skill that operating-point rows of one physical device repeat the geometry columns (done here) or add a `physical_device_id` grouping column.
- Preprint-versus-version-of-record (all five): same as p3_12 proposals (a `numbers_from_version` column; Crossref license never transfers to a preprint-sourced row). shamsansari2021: the batch CSV year (2021) is the preprint year while Crossref issued 2022-04-06 and the journal title differs; the skill should say whether `year` follows the cached version or the version of record.
- Bandwidth with an instrument-attributed limit and a visible dip to -3 dB inside the range (sabatti2024 SH, guo2026-a, shamsansari2021): conventions (c) cover no-crossing bounds but not traces that touch the -3 dB line through noise ripple while the authors claim a bandwidth equal to the limit. Entered as gt (sabatti, guo) or approx (shamsansari2021) following the authors wording; proposal: add a convention for author-claimed bandwidth where the trace is noisy at the limit.
- CLTW parameter semantics (guo2026): the T-rail letters (g, r, c, s, t, h) are not defined in the text and are used with different geometry in other papers; sims/SPEC.md `periodic_t_rail` would need a per-paper letter-to-feature mapping to be usable. Proposal: ask distillers to record the mapping and its source figure in provenance, and mark the paper grade C when the mapping is not unambiguous.
- `max_baud_gbd` for OOK (guo2026): the paper states 100 Gb/s OOK and 56 Gbaud PAM-4 only; baud for OOK was entered as a derived value (1 bit per symbol). Proposal: skill rule for deriving baud from OOK/NRZ line rate, or leave empty.
- Segmented traveling-wave electrodes (shamsansari2021) fit `segmented`; flip-chip laser integration has no `integration` value for a laser-plus-modulator transmitter (entered monolithic with a tag).
- `discovered_via` tokens `web_search`, `author_group_followup`, `continuation_2026_10_02` entered verbatim as in p3_02/p3_12.
- Organization rows copied identically from siblings so merges cannot conflict: City University of Hong Kong (p3_01/p3_11/p3_12), Harvard University and Center for Nanoscale Systems (p3_11), Nokia Bell Labs (p3_04). New here: The Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (parent of the facility, expanded from the paper's HKUST), Nanosystem Fabrication Facility, Freedom Photonics.

## 2026-10-03 audit follow-up (not applied)
- Author-claimed bandwidth at an instrument limit where the trace dips below -3 dB (sabatti2024 SH, shamsansari2021): entered as the authors' value (no `gt` for sabatti2024-sh, `approx` for shamsansari2021) with `bw_measured_to_ghz` = the usable range and the dips written in the evidence note. Proposal: a convention naming this case, or a `bw_basis` value for author-claimed bandwidth.
- `foundry_or_fab` when an acknowledgment names several facilities without assigning layers (sabatti2024): both entered. Proposal: say so in convention (e) or add a column for facilities that supported fabrication.
- Tested-to handling values (guo2026-a 11.5 dBm, "stable up to"): entered `approx` with basis `author_estimate`. Proposal: allow a `gt`-like qualifier meaning "no failure observed up to this value" or a dedicated tested-to column.
