# Q2 audit: E2i.2 + E3i + U3 (2026-10-07)

Method: read DevLog-012, sims/SPEC.md (stage contract, changelog), engine/README.md, the 2026-10-02 Q2 audit, the changed engine
sources (stages.mjs, recess.mjs, config.mjs, run.mjs, materials.mjs, eo-overlap.mjs, rf-line.mjs), tests (eo-rf-runner.test.mjs,
fixture gsg-eo-rf.yaml) and the app sim page, worker and SimStageResults.svelte. Every closed form below was re-derived independently
(Python/Node scratch scripts in the session scratchpad, `scratchpad/q2/`; nothing in the repo was modified except this report).
Paper configs were only used in memory with placeholder declarations as numerical probes; nothing here is a literature validation.

Verdict: the physics cores are correct against independent derivations (rotation sense, Pockels chain, push-pull VpiL, complex gamma,
energy-weighted tan delta, conduction loss, skin effect, Wheeler rule including an independent curved-conductor check). No blocking
error. One numerical finding (arm B is solved on an unrefined electrostatic mesh), two contract findings, eight minor.

## Limitations of this audit

- No mutation testing beyond the author's 13 mutants; the gaps found (N1, C2) are coverage gaps of the analytic fixture (uniform field, no floating conductors).
- Scalar optical limits (audit D5, F1, F3) are unchanged and out of scope; N1 is quantified on one geometry only.
- Wheeler accuracy was checked on plates, mirror variants and one curved conductor; CPW corner singularities were probed only through mesh-scale and step-check sensitivity.
- No paper reproduction is claimed; the lin2025 and chen2022 runs used placeholder loss declarations in memory.
